What it costs

There are three separate costs, and they work differently. Here is the shape of it, so you know what you are being quoted on.

What we are engaged to do

Three kinds of engagement. Only the first has a fixed price.

  • 1. Residential safety and aging-in-place technical audit

    $249

    A flat fee, because it is the same job every time. Somebody comes to the home, works through it room by room, and writes you a report: an action plan within 48 hours and a contractor-ready punch list. You own that report whether or not you buy anything else from us.

    Credited in full against your installation if you go ahead, so the audit effectively costs nothing if you do.

  • 2. Smart home and medical IoT network hardening

    Quoted in your report

    Mesh coverage planned around where devices actually live, local automation, UPS backup, and network segmentation, plus the equipment itself. This is the cost that genuinely varies: a house needing two sensors and a house needing a full system are not comparable.

    An ongoing monthly service covers checking the system is really running, batteries and replacements, updates, reconfiguration, and 24 hour technical support.

  • 3. Business systems consulting

    Quoted per engagement

    Baseline workstation and network security reviews for independent clinics and allied health practices, and technical writing, standard operating procedures and workflow documentation for healthtech companies.

    Scoped on a call first, because the work varies more than anything else here. See services for business.

Why only the audit has a number

The audit is a flat fee because it is the same job every time: a visit and a written report. Equipment is not. A home needing two sensors and a home needing a full system are not comparable, and any number quoted before somebody has seen the place would be a guess dressed up as a quote.

The monthly service depends on what ends up installed. Both numbers are in your written report, before you agree to anything. Business engagements are scoped on a call, for the same reason.

Licensed trades are billed separately

This is the part people are most often surprised by, so it is worth being blunt about. Ageless evaluates the home, writes the specification, and installs low voltage and plug-in technology. Anchoring a grab bar, fitting a handrail, wiring a fixture and any plumbing are carried out by licensed trades, and you pay them directly.

Your punch list gives each of those items a cost range and says whether it is a job for a trade or something the family can do without paying anybody. We do not take a referral fee from any trade, so the ranges are estimates rather than quotes we benefit from.

What we will tell you before you spend anything

  • What the audit costs, before we book it.
  • What the equipment costs, in the written report, before you agree to it.
  • Which items need a licensed trade, with a cost range for each.
  • What you could do yourself for nothing, in that same report.
  • What the monthly service costs, and what happens if you stop it.
All prices are in Canadian dollars.

Ageless does not take a commission from equipment manufacturers. That matters, because it is what makes it possible to recommend against a product, or to tell you that a lightbulb solves your problem.
