Questions and answers

The things people actually ask on the first phone call. If your question is not here, call and ask it.

What does it cost?

Each package has one published price. Installation is included in the package price. Whatever network setup that home needs is part of every package, and it appears as its own named charge at its own published price, so you can see exactly what it is. Call for the network charge. On top of that there is one monthly service price, and it is the same for every customer, whichever package they bought. Call for the monthly service price. No price here begins with the word from, and nothing is left to be worked out later. The figures are all on the pricing page.

What happens first?

It starts with a free twenty minute phone call. You describe the home and what has been on your mind, and we ask about the layout, the daily routine and the internet connection. By the end of the call you know which package suits that home, whether the home needs network work, and the total price, before anybody comes out. Nothing is charged for the call, and you are under no obligation at the end of it.

How do we know which package we need?

Each package is built around one worry, so most families already know which one they are calling about.

  • The Foundation: lighting, locks, leaks, heat, and staying connected.
  • The Daily Routine: the kitchen and fire, and medication.
  • Help When It's Needed: falls, and getting help.
  • Knowing the Day Went Right: activity through the day, and daily check-ins.

If you are not sure, that is what the call is for. There is more detail on the packages page.

Do the packages have to be bought in a set order?

No. The four stand alone and can be bought in any order, one now and another next year, or several at once. Nothing has to be bought first to make anything else work, and the prices do not compound: a second package costs its published price, not more because of what came before it. The monthly service price is the same whether you have one package or all four.

What if my parent does not want any of this?

That happens often, and it is a reasonable reaction. Being told that your home is now a hazard is not a pleasant conversation. We will not install monitoring in a home where the person living there has not agreed to it, even when the family is paying. In practice the objection is usually to being watched rather than to being safer, and there is a lot that can be done without cameras. There is an article on this: how to talk to a parent about safety without making them defensive.

Do you work in my area?

Ottawa and the surrounding region, and Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. If you are near the edge of either, call and we will tell you straight away rather than leaving you guessing.

What if I change my mind?

Then you change your mind, and it costs you nothing. The call is free and commits you to nothing. An installation can be moved or cancelled with a phone call at any point before it happens. The monthly service can be cancelled any time, with no notice period and no fee, and we refund the unused part of the month. A service that is hard to leave is a service people hesitate to start.

What happens if something breaks?

You call, at any hour. Technical support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, remotely or in person. Repairs and replacements are part of what the monthly service is for. That is most of the reason the monthly service exists: a system that is quietly broken is worse than no system, because everybody has stopped thinking about it.

Do you do the installation yourself?

Yes. The person you speak to on the phone is the person who comes to the house and sets everything up. Nothing is handed to a subcontractor who has never spoken to you, and nothing is left half configured for the family to finish.

Is this surveillance? Will there be cameras in the house?

No cameras and no microphones unless a household specifically asks for them, and most do not. The usual approach uses discreet sensors that register patterns, for example that the kettle was used this morning, rather than anything that records what a person is doing. We are explicit about exactly what is and is not collected before anything is installed.

Who gets the alerts?

Whoever the household decides, and it is set up on installation day. It is usually one or two family members. The important part is that alerts reach somebody who can actually act on them, which is why we configure this rather than leaving it on the factory default.

What happens in a medical emergency?

The system contacts emergency services and the people on the household's contact list. Ageless is not an emergency response service and does not answer medical emergencies. Our 24 hour availability is technical support for equipment. In an emergency, call 911.

Are you a home care agency? Can you send someone to help with care?

No. Ageless does not provide personal care or nursing care and does not send caregivers. What we sell is the choosing, the installing and the keeping it working: the package that suits the home, the setup on the day, and the monthly service behind it. If care is what you need, say so on the call and we will point you towards people who provide it.

Can I arrange this for a parent who lives in a different city from me?

Yes, and that is the common case. Most of the people who call are not in the same city as the parent they are calling about. The call can be with you, the installation happens at the parent's home, and keeping the family informed is part of the monthly service, so you do not have to be in the room to know things are working.

We already bought a few things. Is that wasted?

Usually not. Tell us what is already in the house when we speak. Quite often the equipment is fine and the setup is the problem, and looking after equipment that is already there is something the monthly service can take on. If something you own already does part of what a package would have done, we will say so on the call rather than selling it to you twice.

Still have a question?

Ask it on the phone. The call is free, there is no script and no obligation.
