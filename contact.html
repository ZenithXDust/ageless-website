What happens next

  1. We reply within one business day to learn more and answer questions.
  2. We book a visit at a time that works for you and anyone you’d like present.
  3. You receive your written priority action plan within 48 hours of the walk-through.

If you are dealing with a medical emergency or a fall in progress, call 911. Ageless does not provide urgent or clinical care.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the assessment take?

About two hours. It runs longer for a larger home or one with several bathrooms, and it is always paced to you — we take breaks whenever you want one.

When do we get the report?

Within 48 hours of the visit. You receive a written priority action plan by email — or on paper if you prefer — with findings sorted into immediate, 30-day, and long-term, each with an estimated cost and timeframe.

Do you do the installation work?

No. Ageless provides the clinical plan and equipment sourcing. If you’d like, we coordinate with trusted, vetted accessibility contractors and handymen in the Ottawa region — and you are always free to use your own. You approve every step, and nothing proceeds without you.

What does it cost?

The first phone consultation is free. Assessment pricing depends on the size of the home and the number of bathrooms — contact us for current rates. Equipment and any contractor work are quoted separately and are yours to accept or decline.

Do we need to prepare or tidy up?

No — please don’t. We need to see the home the way it is actually lived in. Just have any assistive devices you use (cane, walker, shower chair) on hand so we can see them in place.

What area do you serve?

Ottawa, ON and the surrounding communities, including Kanata, Orléans, Barrhaven, Nepean, and Gloucester. Ask us if you’re just outside that area.
