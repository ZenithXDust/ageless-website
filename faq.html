Questions and answers

The things people actually ask on the first phone call. If your question is not here, call and ask it.

What does it cost?

The assessment is a flat $249, plus tax, and it is credited in full against your installation if you go ahead. Equipment and installation vary, because they depend entirely on the home, and the monthly service depends on what ends up installed. Both of those numbers are in your written report before you agree to anything. There is a fuller explanation on the pricing page.

What actually happens during the assessment?

Somebody comes to the home and goes through it room by room: the kitchen, the stairs, the bathroom, the lighting, the doors, the internet connection, and the daily routine. Nothing is sold on the day. Afterwards you get a written report setting out what was found, what we would recommend, what it would cost, and what you could do yourselves for nothing.

What if my parent does not want any of this?

That happens often, and it is a reasonable reaction. Being told that your home is now a hazard is not a pleasant conversation. We will not install monitoring in a home where the person living there has not agreed to it, even when the family is paying. In practice the objection is usually to being watched rather than to being safer, and there is a lot that can be done without cameras. There is an article on this: how to talk to a parent about safety without making them defensive.

Do you work in my area?

Ottawa and the surrounding region, and Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. If you are near the edge of either, call and we will tell you straight away rather than leaving you guessing.

What if I change my mind?

Then you change your mind, and it costs you nothing. You can cancel or move an assessment at any time before it with a phone call. The monthly service can be cancelled any time, with no notice period and no fee, and we refund the unused part of the month. A service that is hard to leave is a service people hesitate to start.

What happens if something breaks?

You call, at any hour. Technical support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, remotely or in person. Repairs and replacements are part of what the monthly service is for. A safety system that is quietly broken is worse than no system, because everybody has stopped worrying.

Do you do the installation yourself?

Yes. The same person who does the assessment does the installation. Nothing is subcontracted to an installer who has never seen the report.

Is this surveillance? Will there be cameras in the house?

No cameras and no microphones unless a household specifically asks for them, and most do not. The usual approach uses discreet sensors that register patterns, for example that the kettle was used this morning, rather than anything that records what a person is doing. We are explicit about exactly what is and is not collected before anything is installed.

Who gets the alerts?

Whoever the household decides, and it is set up during installation. It is usually one or two family members. The important part is that alerts reach somebody who can actually act on them, which is why we configure this rather than leaving it on the factory default.

What happens in a medical emergency?

The system contacts emergency services and the people on the household's contact list. Ageless is not an emergency response service and does not answer medical emergencies. Our 24 hour availability is technical support for equipment. In an emergency, call 911.

Are you a home care agency? Can you send someone to help with care?

No. Ageless does not provide personal care or nursing care and does not send caregivers. We are the assessment, installation and management layer that sits between families and the technology. If care is what you need, say so on the call and we will point you towards people who provide it.

Can I arrange this for a parent who lives in a different city from me?

Yes, and that is the common case. Most of the people who call are not in the same city as the parent they are calling about. The assessment happens at the parent's home, the report goes to whoever needs it, and the ongoing updates are designed for a family member who is not local.

We already bought a few things. Is that wasted?

Usually not. Bring it up on the call. Part of the assessment is looking at what is already in the house and whether it is working, correctly configured, and actually being used. Quite often the equipment is fine and the setup is the problem.

Still have a question?

Ask it on the phone. There is no script and no obligation.
