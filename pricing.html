What it costs

There are three separate costs, and they work differently. Here is the shape of it, so you know what you are being quoted on.

The three costs

  • The assessment

    A one-time cost. Somebody comes to the home, goes through it properly, and writes you a report. You own that report whether or not you buy anything else from us.

  • Equipment and installation

    A one-time cost, and the one that genuinely varies. A home needing two sensors and a home needing a full system are not comparable, which is why an honest number cannot be given until somebody has seen the place.

  • The monthly service

    An ongoing cost. It covers monitoring that the system is really running, batteries and replacements, updates, reconfiguration, and 24 hour technical support.

Why there are no numbers on this page yet

Ageless is new. Publishing a price before running enough assessments to know what the work actually takes would mean picking a number and hoping. A number that has to be corrected upward later is worse for you than no number today.

So: call and ask. You will get a straight answer for your situation, not a range designed to get you on the phone. The figures will be published here once they are real.

What we will tell you before you spend anything

  • What the assessment costs, before we book it.
  • What the equipment costs, in the written report, before you agree to it.
  • What you could do yourself for nothing, in that same report.
  • What the monthly service costs, and what happens if you stop it.
All prices are in Canadian dollars.

Ageless does not take a commission from equipment manufacturers. That matters, because it is what makes it possible to recommend against a product, or to tell you that a lightbulb solves your problem.
