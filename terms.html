Terms of service

What Ageless does, what it does not do, and what each of us is responsible for. Last updated 26 August 2026.

1. Who we are

Ageless is a home safety assessment, installation and management service operated by Amin Osman, serving Ottawa and Toronto, Ontario. You can reach us at 437-457-5307.

These terms apply when you book, buy, or use any Ageless service. Booking an appointment means you accept them.

2. What Ageless does

Three things:

  • an in-home safety and independence assessment, with a written report
  • sourcing and installing technology suited to that particular home
  • an ongoing monthly service that maintains the system and keeps the household informed

3. What Ageless does not do

This section matters more than any other on this page. Please read it.

Ageless is not an emergency service.

We do not monitor for emergencies and we do not respond to them. Alerts generated by installed equipment are routed to emergency services and to the contacts the household nominates, not to a staffed Ageless desk. In an emergency, call 911.

Ageless does not provide health care. We do not provide nursing care, personal care, medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Nothing we say, write, or recommend is medical advice. Decisions about health, medication and care belong with a physician or another qualified health professional.

Ageless does not provide caregiving. We are not a home care agency and we do not send caregivers.

Ageless does not manufacture equipment. We select, supply, install and maintain equipment made by other companies.

The 24 hour availability is technical support. It covers equipment that has stopped working. It is not a medical or emergency line.

4. Booking appointments

Appointments run Monday to Sunday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM Eastern. A time chosen on our website is a request, not a confirmed booking. An appointment is confirmed when we confirm it with you directly.

You can cancel or move an appointment at any time before it, at no charge. A phone call is enough. We know that plans involving an older parent change at short notice, and we would rather you moved the visit than pushed through a bad day.

5. The assessment and the report

The assessment fee is $249 in Canadian dollars, plus applicable taxes. If you go ahead with an installation, the full assessment fee is credited against it.

The report is our opinion, based on what could be observed in the home on the day of the visit. It is not:

  • a building or home inspection
  • an engineering, electrical or structural report
  • a medical, functional or cognitive assessment
  • a certification that a home is safe

Conditions that were hidden, that we were not shown, or that arise after the visit fall outside its scope. The report is prepared for the household that commissioned it and should not be relied on by anybody else.

6. The agreement of the person living in the home

We will not install monitoring equipment in a home without the informed agreement of the person who lives there, even when somebody else is paying for it.

That person may withdraw their agreement at any time, and we will remove or disable the equipment on request. This applies regardless of who holds the contract or pays the invoices, unless we are presented with valid legal authority, such as a power of attorney or a guardianship order, that says otherwise.

7. Equipment

Equipment becomes yours once it is paid for in full. It is covered by the manufacturer's warranty, not by a warranty from Ageless. We will help you make a warranty claim, but we cannot extend or replace the manufacturer's terms.

Installed equipment depends on things outside our control, including mains power, your internet connection, cellular networks, and the services run by the companies that make it. If any of those fail, the equipment may not work.

8. The monthly service

The monthly service covers:

  • checking that the system is genuinely running
  • batteries, replacements and repairs of equipment we installed
  • software updates and security patches
  • reconfiguration when your internet or phone changes
  • technical support, 24 hours a day, remote or in person
  • a regular plain-language update to the household

It runs month to month with no minimum term. You can end it at any time, with no notice period, and we refund the unused part of the month. If we need to end it, we will give you 30 days notice so you have time to arrange something else.

If it ends, the equipment stays yours, but it stops being maintained by us, and any features that depend on a subscription may stop working.

9. What we cannot promise

Safety technology reduces risk. It does not remove it.

Devices fail. Batteries die. Networks go down. No system detects every event, and no alert is guaranteed to arrive. Ageless does not warrant that any system will detect every incident, or that any alert will reach anybody.

Nobody should treat installed equipment as the only safeguard for a person at risk. It is one layer among several, alongside human contact and proper medical care.

10. Payment

The assessment fee is payable at the time of the visit. Equipment and installation are quoted in the report and payable as agreed before work begins. The monthly service is billed monthly in advance.

All prices are in Canadian dollars and exclude applicable taxes.

11. Cancellation and refunds

  • Assessment. Cancel or move it at any time before the appointment, at no charge. If we arrive and nobody is home and we have not heard from you, we may charge half the fee to cover the trip. If we have to cancel on you, the visit is free when we rebook it.
  • Equipment and installation. Once equipment has been ordered for your home specifically, it may not be returnable. We will always tell you before we order anything.
  • Monthly service. Cancel at any time, with no notice period and no cancellation fee. We refund the unused part of the month you have already paid for.

These terms are deliberately generous. A service that is hard to leave is a service people hesitate to start, and this one is bought by families who are already anxious about committing a parent to anything.

Nothing here removes rights you have under Ontario consumer protection law, which apply regardless of what this page says.

12. Limits on our liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, Ageless is not liable for indirect, incidental or consequential loss, and our total liability for any claim connected to our services is limited to the amount you paid us in the twelve months before the claim arose.

Nothing in these terms limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be excluded. If any part of this section is unenforceable, the rest still applies.

13. Other companies

Some equipment relies on services from other companies, including monitoring providers, app makers and network operators. Those services carry their own terms and privacy practices. We will tell you which ones apply to your system before it is installed.

14. Changes to these terms

We may update this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change materially affects an ongoing monthly service, we will tell you directly rather than relying on you noticing.

15. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada that apply there. Any dispute goes to the courts of Ontario.
