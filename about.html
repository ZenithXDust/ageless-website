About Ageless

Independence, preserved and protected.

Most seniors share a single, clear goal: to remain in their own homes for as long as possible. At Ageless, we believe adapting your house shouldn’t mean sacrificing comfort, dignity, or the feeling of home.

Why an RN-led approach

A home modification is only as good as the understanding behind it. Most accessibility work is sold by contractors who assess the structure. Ageless assesses the person moving through it.

A general contractor asks

Where can a bar be mounted? What will the tile and framing allow? What does code require? These are the right structural questions — but the inquiry stops at the wall.

A Registered Nurse asks

How does this person rise from the toilet? Where does their balance falter mid-turn? What will their reach and stamina look like in three years? The bar then goes where the body actually needs it.

During an assessment we evaluate

Body mechanics

How you transition from sitting to standing — at the chair, the bed, the toilet, the car — and where the environment makes that harder than it needs to be.

Energy conservation

Making daily routines like cooking and bathing less exhausting, so your energy lasts through the day.

Progressive mobility

Anticipating future needs, so adaptations made today still work five years from now.

Ageless is led by Amin Osman, RN — a Registered Nurse who spent years watching preventable falls change lives, and started this practice to work upstream of the hospital.

Who we work with in Ottawa

The assessment is the same rigorous walk-through for everyone. What changes is who we’re reporting to, and what they need from it.

Older adults

You want to stay in your home and make clear-eyed decisions about it. We give you the information and the options; the choices stay yours, always.

Adult children & caregivers

You’re worried about a parent and unsure which risks are urgent. We turn a houseful of concerns into a short, prioritized list the whole family can agree on.

Healthcare partners

Discharge planners, occupational therapists, and case managers: we provide the environmental assessment that complements your clinical plan, usually within days of referral.

48-hour action plan

Book an in-home assessment

One visit of about two hours, in your home, at your pace. Within 48 hours you receive a written action plan — findings sorted into immediate, 30-day, and long-term, each with costs and timeframes. You decide what happens next.
