Services for clinics and healthtech companies

The same two disciplines that go into a home, applied to a practice or a product. Systems reviewed, and documentation somebody will actually follow.

Who this is for

Independent clinics and allied health practices, usually small enough that nobody on staff owns IT, and healthtech companies who need somebody able to read both a network diagram and a care workflow. It is not aimed at hospitals or at organisations that already have their own security function.

This is consulting work, delivered as written findings and documents. It is not a managed service: we do not take over the running of your systems.

What we do

  • Workstation and network baseline review

    A practical look at the things that are usually wrong and rarely examined: how workstations are configured and updated, how accounts and passwords are handled, how the network is laid out, what is exposed to the internet, how backups are taken, and whether anybody has ever tried restoring one.

    You get a written baseline with findings ranked by what would actually hurt, each with a specific remedy and a sense of the effort involved. Written so you can hand it to whoever does your IT, or work through it yourself.

  • Technical writing and procedures

    Standard operating procedures, onboarding and offboarding checklists, incident and escalation steps, and workflow documentation for a product or a practice.

    The useful part is not the writing. It is that the person writing it has sat in the kind of setting being documented. A procedure that ignores how a room actually runs gets ignored back.

What this is not

Worth being exact about, because these words get used loosely and the difference matters when you are the one relying on them.

  • Not a compliance audit or a certification. A baseline review is our assessment of your systems. It certifies nothing, and it is not a legal opinion on your obligations under PHIPA or any other statute.
  • Not penetration testing. We review configuration and design. We do not attack your systems.
  • Not a managed service. There is no monitoring desk and no helpdesk behind this. We review, document, and hand it over.
  • Not clinical or professional advice. Documenting a workflow is a description of how your practice operates. It is not advice about the care delivered inside it.

Who does the work

Amin Osman, Founder and Lead Consultant. He holds a BScN and is certified in CompTIA A+, Network+ and Security+, and has worked in in-home care and in crisis intervention. Ageless is a one person company today, so the person who scopes the work is the person who does it.

What it costs

Quoted per engagement, after a scoping call. The work varies too much for a rate card to be honest, and a number published before anybody has seen your setup would be a guess dressed up as a quote. The call itself costs nothing.
