About Ageless

Ageless exists because the job of keeping somebody safely at home needs two kinds of knowledge, and they almost never come together.

Amin Osman

Ageless was founded and is run by Amin Osman. He holds a BScN and is certified in CompTIA A+, Network+ and Security+. He has worked in in-home care and in crisis intervention, with his work focused on older adults.

That combination is the whole reason this company exists. Home safety technology usually gets handled by one of two groups. Care people understand the person but cannot configure a network, so the equipment ends up half working. Technology people can configure anything but have never sat in a kitchen with a family who are frightened and disagreeing with each other. Both halves are needed on the same visit.

Ageless is a one person company today. When you call, you reach Amin. More people are intended as the work grows, and this page will say so when it happens rather than implying a team that does not exist.

What we believe

Staying home is usually the better outcome

Not always, and we will say so when it is not. But moving somebody out of a home they know is a serious thing, and it is often done earlier than it needed to be, because nobody knew what the alternatives were.

The older adult is the client, not the subject

The person living in the house gets a say in everything installed in it. We will not fit monitoring in a home where the person living there has not agreed to it, even when the family is paying and even when the family disagrees. That rule costs us work occasionally. It stays.

Fear is a bad way to sell anything

You will not find a frightening statistic on this website. The risks are real and stating them plainly is enough. Anybody who needs to scare you into buying safety equipment is telling you something about the equipment.

Installing it is the easy part

Anyone can fit a sensor. Making sure it is still working in fourteen months, after a router was replaced and a battery died and a phone was upgraded, is the actual service. That is why there is a monthly component rather than a one-time sale.

Where we work

Ottawa and Toronto, Ontario. If you are just outside either, call and ask rather than assuming the answer is no.
