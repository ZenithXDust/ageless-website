Comprehensive in-home safety assessments

We bring clinical expertise directly to your living room. Our core service is a thorough, room-by-room evaluation of your environment to identify hidden risks and create a concrete, personalized plan for your continued independence.

Our three-step assessment process

  1. The pre-assessment conversation

    Before we inspect a single room, we sit down with you to understand your daily routines, your definition of independence, and your specific goals. You are the expert on your life; we are here to support it.

  2. The clinical walk-through

    We evaluate high-risk zones through a nursing lens. We measure clearances, test ambient lighting, and assess mobility transitions across your exterior entryways, stairways, bathrooms, and kitchen. We look at property conditions, not personal limitations.

  3. The priority action plan

    Within 48 hours, you receive a clear, documented summary matrix. This plan categorizes our findings into immediate priorities, 30-day recommendations, and long-term considerations — keeping you in complete control of what happens next.

Adaptive equipment consulting

We focus on practical, high-impact, and cost-effective solutions that integrate seamlessly into your daily life — without making your house feel like a hospital. Our tailored recommendations often include:

  • Stability

    Clinically positioned grab bars and stability rails, anchored to hold real weight.

  • Seating

    Specialized seating-height adjustments — typically 17 to 19 inches — for chairs, the bed, and the toilet.

  • Lighting

    High-contrast and motion-activated lighting designed for aging eyes and night-time routes.

  • Transitions

    Stair-rail extensions and slip-resistant threshold transitions at doorways and level changes.

  • Water safety

    Water-temperature regulation and anti-scald devices at the tub, shower, and sink.

Trusted implementation

Ageless provides the clinical blueprint and equipment sourcing. When your custom action plan calls for physical modifications, we can connect you with trusted, vetted accessibility contractors and handymen here in the Ottawa region, so everything is installed safely and securely.

You are never obligated to use a referral. The plan is yours to implement however you choose.
