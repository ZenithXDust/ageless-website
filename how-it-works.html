How it works

Three parts. You choose a package, we install it, and we keep it working. Here is what each one actually involves, so there are no surprises.

Step one: you choose, and it costs nothing

The first step is a free twenty minute phone call. There is no charge for it, no visit to pay for first, and no obligation at the end of it.

We ask about:

  • the kitchen, and how cooking actually happens day to day
  • stairs, thresholds, and the routes walked most often
  • lighting, especially between the bed and the bathroom at night
  • doors, locks, and who currently has a key
  • what has already gone wrong, or nearly gone wrong
  • the internet connection, because nearly all of this depends on it
  • the daily routine, which usually matters more than the building

At the end of the call you are told which package suits that home, or that none of them does. You are also told the total, including whether that home needs network work, before anybody comes out.

Some homes need network work first and some do not. We ask two or three questions on the call and tell you the total before anybody comes out.

We do not read a catalogue down the phone, and we will say when the answer is a lightbulb and a free afternoon rather than a package.

Step two: installation day

We order the equipment, bring it, and install it. We set up the accounts and the apps, test everything against how that household actually lives, and show whoever needs to know how it works. We take the packaging away with us.

Installation is included. We fit it, set it up and test it, and we take the packaging away with us.

Nothing gets installed that was not agreed on the call first. If we arrive and something does not suit the house after all, we stop and talk to you rather than pressing on.

Ageless fits low voltage and plug-in technology itself. Anchoring a grab bar, fitting a handrail, wiring a fixture and anything involving plumbing are carried out by licensed trades, who bill you directly. We tell you which is which before you buy, not on the day.

Step three: we keep it running

This is the part you are actually buying.

Anybody can order a sensor online. Almost nobody can keep four of them working for three years while an internet provider changes, a phone gets replaced, and a battery quietly dies in a hallway nobody walks down in daylight.

Technology that nobody maintains stops working, and it usually stops quietly. A dead battery does not announce itself. A sensor that dropped off the network six weeks ago looks exactly like a sensor that has had nothing to report. This is the part most families do not think to ask about, and it is the part that decides whether any of this is still working in a year.

The monthly service covers:

  • checking that the system is genuinely running, not just installed
  • batteries, replacements and repairs
  • software updates and security patches
  • reconfiguration when the internet or the phone changes
  • a regular plain-language update, so the family is not left guessing
  • technical support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, remote or in person

Call for the monthly price

One price per household, whatever you bought. A home with one package and a home with all four pay the same, because what is being paid for is somebody keeping an eye on it rather than the hardware.

If you stop it

You can cancel at any time, with no notice period and no fee, and we refund the unused part of the month. The equipment stays yours. It stops being maintained by us, and any features that depend on a subscription may stop working, which we will tell you about before you cancel rather than after.

What Ageless is not

Ageless is not an emergency service.

If something happens, the system contacts emergency services and the people on the household's contact list. The 24 hour availability is technical support: if a device fails at two in the morning, that is what we handle. In a medical emergency, call 911.

We do not send caregivers. Ageless is not a home care agency. We do not provide personal care or nursing care, and we will happily point you towards people who do.

We do not manufacture equipment. We choose it, sell it, install it, and keep it working. We are not tied to one manufacturer, so we can tell you a product is wrong for the home, which a company selling only its own product cannot really do.

Start with the call

Twenty minutes, no charge, and it ends with which package suits and what it would cost. Everything after that is your decision.
