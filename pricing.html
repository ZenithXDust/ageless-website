Every package works on its own. Buy one, or several, in whatever order suits. There is no package you have to buy first, and if the home needs network work that is a separate named item on the price list rather than another package.

Why there is no "from" price on this page

A price that starts with the word "from" is not a price, it is an opening position. Families buying this are already anxious, and most of them have been quoted that way before by somebody else.

So the package prices are fixed. The one thing that genuinely varies between houses, whether the network needs work before any of this is reliable, is a separate named line with its own fixed price rather than a range hidden inside the package. On the call we ask two or three questions, work out which applies, and tell you the total.

One monthly price, whatever you bought

A home with one package and a home with all four pay the same monthly amount. What is being paid for is somebody keeping an eye on it, not the amount of hardware in the house.

It can be cancelled at any time, with no notice period and no fee, and we refund the unused part of the month. What the monthly service covers.

Licensed trades are billed separately

This is the part people are most often surprised by, so it is worth being blunt about. Ageless installs the low voltage and plug-in technology in these packages. Anchoring a grab bar, fitting a handrail, wiring a fixture and any plumbing are carried out by licensed trades, and you pay them directly.

If a package needs any of that, we tell you on the call, with a cost range for each item and whether it is a job for a trade or something the family can do without paying anybody.

What we will tell you before you spend anything

  • What the package costs, before you agree to it.
  • Whether that home needs network work, and what it costs, on the call.
  • Which items need a licensed trade, with a cost range for each.
  • What you could do yourself for nothing, in that same conversation.
  • What the monthly service costs, and what happens if you stop it.

That fourth one is not a throwaway. Some of the most useful changes in a home cost nothing at all, and we would rather tell you than sell you something instead.

How Ageless makes its money.

Ageless is a retailer. We buy the equipment and sell it to you as part of a package, and what we make on it is inside the package price. No manufacturer pays us to recommend their product.
