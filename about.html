About Ageless

Independence, preserved and protected.

Most seniors share a single, clear goal: to remain in their own homes for as long as possible. At Ageless, we believe that staying at home shouldn’t mean compromising on safety, and adapting your house shouldn’t mean losing its warmth. We specialize in practical, high-impact safety solutions that protect your mobility and preserve your dignity.

Meet your consultant: Amin Osman, RN

I am a Registered Nurse and the founder of Ageless, proudly serving the Ottawa community. Throughout my nursing career, I have seen firsthand how a single, preventable fall can permanently alter a person’s trajectory. I started Ageless because I wanted to move “upstream” — to identify and solve environmental hazards before a hospital visit happens.

The RN advantage

When you hire a contractor, they look at your walls and plumbing. When you work with an RN, you get a clinical perspective on how the human body interacts with its environment. During an Ageless assessment, we evaluate:

Body mechanics

How you transition from sitting to standing — at the chair, the bed, the toilet, and the car — and where the environment makes that harder than it needs to be.

Energy conservation

Making daily routines like cooking and bathing less exhausting, so your energy lasts through the day.

Progressive mobility

Anticipating future needs, so adaptations made today will still work five years from now.

Our philosophy

We never use phrases like “for someone your age.” We don’t judge, and we don’t take over. You are the expert on your own home and your own goals. Our job is simply to provide the clinical insight and adaptive-equipment recommendations — like precisely placed grab bars, tailored seating heights, and hazard removal — that empower you to navigate your space with total confidence.

Let’s secure your space.

Whether you are planning ahead for yourself or looking out for a parent here in the Ottawa area, Ageless is here to help. Contact us today to schedule your comprehensive in-home safety assessment.
