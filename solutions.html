Packages

Organised by what families actually worry about, not by product category. Nobody goes shopping for a stove shutoff. They worry about a fire.

How buying works

Every package works on its own. Buy one, or several, in whatever order suits. Each one includes the setup that home needs to make it work, so there is nothing you have to buy first.

The network charge

A safety device that cannot reach the network is not a safety device. Some homes already have coverage where it matters, at the back of the house, in the shower, out in the garden. Some do not, and that has to be sorted before anything else is worth installing.

Some homes need network work first and some do not. We ask two or three questions on the call and tell you the total before anybody comes out.

It is a named item with its own price, not something added to an invoice afterwards. You will know which applies to your parent's home before you agree to anything.

Ordering happens by phone.

There is no basket and no checkout on this site, so please do not go looking for one. Ageless is one person, and a package is chosen by talking through the house for twenty minutes rather than by clicking. Call 437-457-5307 and we will work out which of these suits, or whether none of them does.

The four packages

The Foundation

The worry

Lighting, locks, water and heat. The unglamorous ones, and often the ones that give the most back for the least money. Isolation belongs here too, because it is a health problem rather than a soft one, and it makes every other risk harder to spot when there is nobody in regular contact to notice a change.

What is included

Lighting on the route from the bed to the bathroom, entry that a family member or a paramedic can use without breaking a door, sensors for leaks and for the heating failing in January, and video calling that works without a password and a menu.

What we do

This is the package where we tell you what to do yourself. Some of it is a lightbulb and a free afternoon, and we would rather say that than quote you for it. The rest we fit, and then we keep it working, which is the actual difficulty with anything that has to work on the first try every time.

The Daily Routine

Call for pricing

The worry

Cooking is the most common way a safe home becomes an unsafe one. A pot left on a back burner, a tea towel too close to the element, a smoke alarm that nobody can hear from the bedroom. Medication is the quieter risk and often the more consequential one: a dose missed, a dose doubled, or a prescription that changed and did not make it into the routine.

What is included

A stove monitor that can cut power to a hob left on with nobody in the room, heat and smoke sensors linked so an alert reaches a family member as well as sounding in the house, and a dispenser that releases only the dose that is due and says something when it has not been taken.

What we do

We look at how cooking actually happens in that kitchen before installing anything, because a shutoff that trips during a normal Sunday dinner will be unplugged within a fortnight. We set the dispenser up around the real prescription and the real routine rather than a default schedule, and we sort it out when the prescription changes, which it will.

Help When It's Needed

Call for pricing

The worry

A fall itself is often survivable. The damage usually comes from the time spent on the floor afterwards, unable to reach a phone.

What is included

A wearable button, automatic fall detection that works without anybody having to press anything, and the range tested in the places falls actually happen, including the shower and the back garden.

What we do

We match the device to the person honestly. A pendant that gets left on the dresser protects nobody, so if that is the likely outcome we will say so and point you at the next package instead of selling you this one.

Knowing the Day Went Right

Call for pricing

The worry

Most families do not want surveillance. They want to know that the day went roughly the way it usually goes, without phoning three times to check and making everybody feel watched.

What is included

Discreet sensors that learn the ordinary pattern, the kettle used in the morning, the fridge opened, movement between rooms, and tell somebody when that pattern breaks. No cameras and no microphones.

What we do

We set the thresholds with the older adult, not behind their back. We are clear about exactly what is and is not recorded, and we will not install this in a home where the person living there has not agreed to it.
