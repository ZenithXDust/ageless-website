Privacy policy

What this website collects, which is almost nothing, and how Ageless handles the information you give us on the phone.

This policy is a draft and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Ageless handles information about people's homes and health, which is governed in Canada by privacy legislation. This page will be replaced with a reviewed version before it should be relied on.

This website

This site collects nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising network, no social media embed, and no cookie set by this site. There is no contact form, so there is nothing on these pages that transmits anything about you anywhere.

The booking page keeps your choices in your own browser only, for as long as the page is open, so that it can show them back to you. Nothing is sent to Ageless until you choose to pick up the phone.

Your internet provider and the company hosting this site will keep ordinary web server records, in the same way as for any website you visit.

When you call us

To do the work, Ageless needs to know things about a household: names, an address, a phone number, the layout of the home, and something about the daily routine and health situation of the person living there.

We use that information to:

  • carry out the assessment and write the report
  • recommend, source and install equipment
  • provide the monthly service and technical support
  • contact the people a household has asked us to contact

We do not sell it, rent it, or share it for marketing. Equipment suppliers and monitoring services necessarily receive what they need to provide their part of the service, and nothing beyond that.

Your choices

  • You can ask what information Ageless holds about you.
  • You can ask for it to be corrected.
  • You can ask for it to be deleted when the service ends.
  • The person living in the home can withdraw agreement to monitoring at any time, including when a family member is paying for it.
