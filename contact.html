Reaching us

  • When to call

    Monday to Sunday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM Eastern, for anything to do with booking, questions, or quotes.

    Technical support, for existing clients whose equipment has stopped working, is available 24 hours a day.

  • Where we work

    Ottawa and the surrounding region, and Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. If you are near the edge of either, call and we will tell you straight away.

  • What happens next

    We talk through the situation, usually for fifteen or twenty minutes. If an assessment makes sense we book one. If it does not, we will tell you that instead.

    If you leave a message, you will hear back within one business day.

In a medical emergency, call 911.

Ageless is not an emergency service. Our 24 hour line is for equipment that has stopped working, not for medical situations.
