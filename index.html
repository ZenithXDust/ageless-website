What families call us about

  • The stove left on

    A burner stays lit after dinner. Nothing happens most nights. It is the one night that everybody is quietly thinking about.

  • A dose missed, or taken twice

    Pills are easy to lose track of. From another city there is no way to know whether Tuesday went the way it was meant to.

  • A fall, and nobody knows

    Most falls are survivable. The lasting harm usually comes from how long somebody waits on the floor before anyone finds out.

How it works

  1. Assess

    We come to the home and go through it room by room. You get a written report of what we found and what we would do about it.

  2. Install

    We source the equipment that suits that house and install it ourselves. Nothing is left in a box for you to work out later.

  3. Manage

    A monthly service keeps it all running, and keeps the family informed without anybody having to chase for an update.

Why Ageless, and not a box from an online shop

  • We assess before we sell anything

    The right equipment depends on the house and the person. A bungalow with a steep back step is a different problem from a third floor apartment. We look first, then recommend.

  • Healthcare and technology in one person

    Amin Osman holds a BScN and is certified in CompTIA A+, Network+ and Security+. This work usually falls to care people who cannot configure the technology, or technology people who have never sat with a worried family.

  • We are still here after installation day

    Equipment fails, internet providers change, batteries die. Technical support is available 24 hours a day, remote or on site.

Who runs Ageless

Ageless was founded and is run by Amin Osman. He holds a BScN and is certified in CompTIA A+, Network+ and Security+. He has worked in in-home care and in crisis intervention, with his work focused on older adults.

Ageless is a one person company today. When you call, you reach Amin. He intends to bring on more people as the work grows, and this page will say so when it happens.
