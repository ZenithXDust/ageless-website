Pause, Verify, Protect

A free community talk on spotting scams before they cost you. No phone needed, no computer needed, and no experience with either.

Call 437-457-5307 to book a talk

Nothing is sold at a talk, and no products are promoted.

Ageless does sell things. Not here. There is no sales pitch at the end, nothing to sign up for, no list to join, and nobody will ask for your telephone number. If a talk ever feels like an advertisement, we have done it badly and we would like to know.

The talk

Scams aimed at older adults have become good. The message that looks like it came from a bank, the phone call about a grandchild in trouble, the invoice for something never ordered. They work because they are built to create hurry, and hurry is the thing that defeats good judgement.

The talk is built around three habits that work whatever the scam is: pause before doing anything, verify by contacting the organisation yourself on a number you already had, and protect what matters most.

The talk takes about an hour, and we stay afterwards for as long as there are questions.

What you need to bring

Nothing at all. You do not need a phone, you do not need a computer, and you do not need to have used either. Anybody who has ever been made to feel foolish by a screen is exactly who this is for.

Everything is in large print, and you take it home with you. There is nothing to write down and nothing to remember on the day.

When they run

Talks are arranged with the place that hosts them, so ask at your library or centre, or call us and we will tell you what is coming up.

The hands-on series

A longer course for anybody who wants to use their own phone or tablet with more confidence. It covers:

  • smartphone basics
  • email
  • video calling
  • photos
  • everyday apps
  • banking safely

The series runs over several sessions. Call and we will tell you how many and how often, so you know what you are committing to before you start.

Capped at ten people.

Every activity happens on the participant's own device, not on a screen at the front of the room. That only works if there is enough of us to go round, so the cap is real rather than a way of making it sound exclusive. Bring the phone or tablet you actually use.

It is free, like the talk. Nothing is sold at these either.

For libraries, seniors centres and retirement residences

If you run a place where older adults already gather, we will come to you. You do not need a technology budget, a partnership agreement, or anything on paper. We are not looking for referrals and we will not be pitching your residents.

What we need from you

  • a room
  • chairs
  • a date

That is the whole list. We bring the printed material, and we bring enough of it. If your room has a screen we can use it, and if it does not, the talk works without one.
