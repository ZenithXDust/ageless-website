What families call us about

  • The stove left on

    A burner stays lit after dinner. Nothing happens most nights. It is the one night that everybody is quietly thinking about.

  • A dose missed, or taken twice

    Pills are easy to lose track of. From another city there is no way to know whether Tuesday went the way it was meant to.

  • A fall, and nobody knows

    Most falls are survivable. The lasting harm usually comes from how long somebody waits on the floor before anyone finds out.

How it works

  1. Choose

    a free twenty minute phone call that ends with which package suits the house and what the total is. Nothing is charged for it.

  2. We install

    We order it, bring it, fit it and set it up. Nothing is left in a box for you to work out later. Structural and electrical work is specified by us and carried out by licensed trades.

  3. We keep it running

    The part that decides whether any of it still works in a year. Batteries, updates, repairs, and a plain update to the family without anybody having to chase for one.

We install what we sell. Licensed trades do the rest.

Ageless fits the low voltage and plug-in technology in these packages itself. Anchoring a grab bar into a stud, fitting a handrail, wiring a fixture and anything involving plumbing are quoted and completed by the appropriate licensed trade, who bills you directly. We will tell you which is which before you buy.

Why Ageless, and not a box from an online shop

  • We choose it, and we say no

    The right equipment depends on the house and the person. A bungalow with a steep back step is a different problem from a third floor apartment. We will tell you when a package is wrong for your parent, and when a lightbulb would do instead.

  • Two disciplines, one person

    Amin Osman holds a BScN and is certified in CompTIA A+, Network+ and Security+. The health sciences background is why the questions about a hallway at three in the morning get asked. The networking certifications are why the pendant still has signal when it does.

  • We are still here after installation day

    This is the actual product. Equipment fails, internet providers change, batteries die, and the monthly service is what catches that. Technical support is available 24 hours a day, remote or on site. That is technical support, not an emergency line.

Who runs Ageless

Ageless was founded and is run by Amin Osman, Founder and Lead Consultant. He holds a BScN and is certified in CompTIA A+, Network+ and Security+. He has worked in in-home care and in crisis intervention, with his work focused on older adults.

What Ageless sells is technology for the home, the work of installing it, and the service that keeps it running. Not care. Ageless does not provide nursing care, medical advice or therapy of any kind, and does not send caregivers.

Ageless is a one person company today. When you call, you reach Amin. He intends to bring on more people as the work grows, and this page will say so when it happens.

Free talks in the community

Ageless runs free community talks and hands-on workshops teaching older adults to use technology confidently, starting with how to spot a scam before it costs you. Nothing is sold at them and no products are promoted.

They are separate from everything else on this page, and that is the point of them. Read about the talks and the hands-on series, or if you run a library, a seniors centre or a residence, ask us to come to you.
